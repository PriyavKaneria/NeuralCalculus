i'm still thinking how the money redistribution will scale out after a few yrs once all the AI investments settle at their peak. (yes, there is no bubble)
when all the trillions of salaries in the IT industry will be replaced by compute, the money that used to get redistributed from big tech to people will get reversed and every player, small or big will be paying the big tech. Other than the one off physical and maintenance investments there will be hardly any arrow in the reverse direction.
The only hope is that VC funding will scale up at an unprecedented level, thriving a large majority in the industry to freelance and or start up. Every saas will feel the pressure to pay to win and the mere few who will lead the taste, creativity and decision making will be harder to find.
