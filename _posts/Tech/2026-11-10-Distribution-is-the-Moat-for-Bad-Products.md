I don't care if I don't raise money, get a lot of funding, scale things before they exist, market demo videos or hype people before it works. ALL I CARE ABOUT IS THAT one day after all the effort, it works for me and a few dozen people I care about and it saves time and gives them joy or time to spend with family. very unpopular opinion but selling is actually easier than building actually good products. many will say it's cope but I say it's statistics. what is more of in the market — extremely viral and high distribution product demo that do not ever get shipped vs products that stick with customers and are actually deemed useful? how many YC companies have successfull demos? almost all. how many of them survive 5yrs in the market? almost none. those that do would have done otherwise as well because the success comes from the founder and the product, a product that evolves with the dynamic market requirements, a founder that is ready to accept that their product is the worst and stupid and does not work and redo everything from scratch. all this is already known by every person who is building a company, but the distractions of the processes are too much for a founder to build than rather to sell as the later brings money. building is research that burns money to keep the idea factory running. everyone who has ever played a good game knows the skill tree cannot be advanced and new products cannot be built without first getting enough research points. so take your time, get the points, get the product working, have a high bar for quality and let the product do your selling
