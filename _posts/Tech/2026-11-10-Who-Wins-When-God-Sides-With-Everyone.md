It'll soon become a fight
A competition for an idea to become a reality
Your idea struck to a dozen people globally
Discipline and efforts USED to decide who scores most and wins
No longer
There will be people with a lot of money to spend on stage now
Those with access to AI far smarter at many things than any human alive
They won't spend time and effort as much as you
They'll catch up fast and are ready to ship slop
Too late and the market is no longer interested because of slop
It's the most fun time to be a builder and an even fun challenge to build something that wins the race
Use whatever you have or can and make sure your legs keep the pace
And remember, it is way more important to try and reach the finish line than to win because it's the judges that give the final score and not a race.
