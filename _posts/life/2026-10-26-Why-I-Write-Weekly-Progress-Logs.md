I've been putting weekly logs for more than 2 years now. everyone asks me why did I start this?
- realised that I don't have many private secrets as most people
- always wanted a diary of sorts to look back but daily is too frequent to commit to
- wanted to really focus on how I use my nerd polymath knowledge instead of just consuming the same (hence the format)
- a public diary of sorts gives accountability and access of the information to future curious people
- I made long streaks before so I knew doing something consistently gives lots of free good dopamine and time to retrospect every week to make the next week better
- just like money, knowledge especially in written compounds. these logs are the SIP to my biography

even without a reason to start, – to start – and to stay is important
